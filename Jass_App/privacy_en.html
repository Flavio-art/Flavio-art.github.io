Privacy Policy

App: Jass Coiffeur & Schieber · Last updated: 25 November 2026

This privacy policy describes how the app "Jass Coiffeur & Schieber" (the "App") handles data. Protecting your data matters to us. The App does not ask for information such as your name, email or location. For the Jass League leaderboard and for playing online with friends, the data needed for these features is transmitted (see Section 3). To improve the App, anonymous usage statistics are also collected (see Section 4).

1. No registration

No registration and no user account are required. The App does not ask for information such as your real name, email address or location. A player name entered in the App stays on your device – except when you play online with friends (Section 3.2) and, in shortened form, for the Jass League (Section 3.3).

2. Local storage

Game states, scores and settings are stored exclusively locally on your device. Your Jass League rating is also calculated on the device. This data does not leave your device – except for the features described in Section 3. Uninstalling the App removes the local data.

3. Online play and Jass League (Firebase)

For online play and the Jass League leaderboard we use Google Firebase Authentication and the Firebase Realtime Database (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA; database location: Belgium).

3.1 Anonymous sign-in

When you use one of these features, the App signs in anonymously. This creates a random identifier (no email, no phone number, no Google account). It is only used so that you can edit your own table or leaderboard entry.

3.2 Playing online with friends

At a table, your player name, the table code and the course of the game are transmitted and shown to the other players at that table. Tables are deleted automatically after the game ends or at the latest after 6 hours of inactivity (Wunschkarte: 24 hours).

3.3 Jass League (leaderboard)

After your first rated game you automatically take part in the Jass League. Your name there is your player name in shortened form (first name, or first name with an initial, e.g. "Flavio C."); if none is set, a randomly generated nickname (e.g. "Trumpf-Fuchs 27"). After 5 games, this name, your rating, the number of rated games and the points gained in the current week appear on the leaderboard, which is visible to all users of the App.

You can change the name at any time. Please do not use your full real name. Tapping "Leave the leaderboard" deletes your entry immediately, and you will not be added again automatically. Inappropriate names can be reported by others; only the anonymous identifier of the reporting person is stored. Reported names are hidden and may be removed by us.

The legal basis for online play is your consent by using the feature (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR / Swiss revFADP). For the leaderboard it is our legitimate interest in a fair comparison of players (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / Swiss revFADP); you can leave at any time.

4. Anonymous usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

To improve the App we use Google Firebase Analytics (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland, and Google LLC, USA). Only anonymous, aggregated usage data is collected, in particular:

  • which game type is started and finished (e.g. Schieber, Differenzler, Coiffeur),
  • the score achieved in a game,
  • a random, anonymous app-instance identifier and basic technical data (e.g. device model, operating-system version, approximate region).

This data is not linked to your identity, does not allow any conclusions about individual persons and is not used for cross-app tracking or advertising. No advertising identifiers (e.g. IDFA) are used. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in anonymous reach and usage measurement (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / Swiss FADP). More information about Google's processing: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. No advertising

The App contains no advertising and no third-party services other than the Firebase services described in Sections 3 and 4.

6. Share and file features

If you share content within the App or import/export a file, this happens only on your explicit action and through your device's standard functions. No data is transmitted to us in the process.

7. Children

The App is suitable for a general audience. For the leaderboard we recommend that everyone – especially children – uses a fantasy nickname instead of their real name.

8. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated when necessary. Changes are published on this page and the date above is updated accordingly.
